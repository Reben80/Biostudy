How to study

Show
  1. One topic at a time. Pick a topic and do a round of multiple choice to learn the terms.
  2. Then type it. Switch to "Type it" for the same topic. Recalling a word is harder than picking it, and that is what makes it stick.
  3. Redo what you missed. At the end, tap "Practice the missed" until the round is perfect.
  4. Flip it around. In multiple choice, tick "Show the term, pick the definition" so you know both directions.
  5. Short sessions, spread out. Fifteen minutes a day for several days beats one long night before the exam.
  6. Mix it up before the test. The day before, choose "Everything" so the terms come in random order like a real exam.

Easy to mix up: leptin vs. ghrelin, rods vs. cones, sympathetic vs. parasympathetic, agonist vs. antagonist, absolute vs. difference threshold, and volley vs. frequency vs. place theory.
